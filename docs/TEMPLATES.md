# Product templates

Each product can use any of these templates. Choose one under **Products → (product) → Theme template**. All of them use the same `main-product` section and theme blocks, so you can rearrange, add or remove blocks freely in the Theme Editor.

| Template | Best for | Layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `product` (default) | Regular catalog products | Header (eyebrow, title, rating, subtitle), gallery, price, variants, quantity, delivery estimate, buy buttons, COD and WhatsApp notices, trust badges, collapsible description, FAQ accordion. Then a trust bar and recommendations. |
| `product.cod-landing` | Hero products with a long sales page | Header; buy column with stock and details row, offer stack, variant cards, delivery estimate, buy buttons, payment methods, WhatsApp notice, guarantee and tabs. Then anchor navigation, scrolling text, editorial hero, benefits, comparison, image with text, timeline, "how COD works" steps, FAQ, final CTA, disclaimer and recommendations. |
| `product.cod-minimal` | Fast, simple pages and paid traffic | Title and price, variants, buy buttons, WhatsApp line, trust row, description. Then a short FAQ. Thumbnails sit below the gallery. |
| `product.cod-video` | Products that sell through demos | A video strip in the buy column, video autoplay in the gallery, then video reels, image with text, COD steps, FAQ and final CTA. |
| `product.cod-offer` | Promotions and bundles | Countdown (hidden until you set a date), offer stack, variant cards, guarantee. Then a trust bar, what's included, comparison, COD steps, FAQ and a guarantee banner. |

## Header area

The title area at the top of the product section is a fixed (static) block called **Product header**. It shows above the gallery on mobile and at the top of the info column on desktop. You can add any blocks to it: badge, title, rating, subtitle, price, apps and more.

## Placeholder content

Templates ship with Spanish placeholder copy that is easy to spot, for example "Beneficio uno", "Escribe aquí tus tiempos reales de entrega." and "Describe aquí tu política…". Replace it with real, accurate information before publishing. The theme does not include sample reviews, statistics or results.

## Anchor links

Long-form buttons link to `#buy` by default, which scrolls to the buy column of the product section. Sections have an **Anchor ID** setting. The anchor navigation section links to `beneficios`, `comparacion`, `pago-contra-entrega` and `faq`. If you change an anchor ID, update the matching navigation link.

## Creating your own templates

In the Theme Editor, open any product template and choose **Create template** from the template dropdown. Base it on one of the COD templates, change the sections, and assign it to products.
